## 4. Core coffee product cards

### 4.1 Caffe Macchiato — Original

- **Identity:** Flavored Luxe Slim Caffe Macchiato coffee mix, original/caffeinated version.
- **Manufacturer-named ingredients:** Hydrolyzed marine collagen, reduced L-glutathione, L-carnitine, green coffee, guarana, *Garcinia cambogia* (manufacturer's typo “Garcia Cambogia” normalized here). Full ingredient panel and individual quantities were **not** verified.
- **Manufacturer's marketed features (not established clinical outcomes):** non-acidic, keto-friendly, appetite and weight-management positioning, skin-brightening/beauty positioning, digestion support. These need qualification and should not become promises to customers.
- **Format:** Official pouch product page says **8 sachets × 21g**; 500g jar also available, plus 1kg pouch.
- **Official reference prices:** pouch **₱399**, half-kilo jar **₱999**, 1kg pouch **₱1,899**.
- **Taste description:** Macchiato-style coffee flavor. Customer reviews from TikTok Shop commonly mention taste, but sweetness/creaminess is subjective.
- **Important:** Guarana/coffee ingredients can contribute caffeine. Do not present Original as caffeine-free or suitable for everyone.
- **Links:** https://luxebeautywellness.com/product/caffe-macchiato-pouch/ ; https://luxebeautywellness.com/product/caffe-macchiato-half-kilo/ ; https://luxebeautywellness.com/product/caffe-macchiato-kilo-pouch/

**Approved natural seller line:** “Mga sis, eto yung Luxe Caffe Macchiato Original—para sa mahilig sa coffee flavor na may collagen at glutathione na ingredients. May sachet pouch tayo, meron ding half-kilo jar. Check n'yo lang yung naka-pin na variant para tama yung price.”

- **Packaging image:** 500g jar; image text mentions collagen, glutathione, chitosan, inulin fiber and L-carnitine.
- **Official price references:** pouch **₱399** (some indexed sale displays ₱379); 500g jar **₱999** (sometimes ₱949 sale); 1kg pouch **₱1,899** (sale may vary).
- **Distinction:** Decaf does **not** mean guaranteed absolute zero caffeine. It also does not establish that the product is safe for pregnancy, breastfeeding, children, or a specific medical condition.
- **Links:** https://luxebeautywellness.com/product/caffe-macchiato-decaf-pouch/ ; https://luxebeautywellness.com/product/caffe-macchiato-decaf-half-kilo/ ; https://luxebeautywellness.com/product/caffe-macchiato-decaf-kilo-pouch/

## 8. Preparation and serving

- The inspected official product pages do **not** consistently supply a definitive water-to-powder ratio for each SKU. Ask buyer to follow the **mixing directions on their actual pouch/jar**.
- **Hot or iced:** The sampled TikTok Macchiato listing says either is possible; safe generic advice: fully dissolve according to label, then add ice if desired.
- **Number of scoops, water volume, daily limit, timing, and permitted users:** Do **not** invent. Retrieve from label before explaining.
- **Storage:** Keep packaging sealed and use its storage instructions, best-before date, lot details, and physical condition.
- **Allergens:** Original Macchiato lists **marine collagen**; the decaf lists **chitosan**. Anyone with fish/shellfish sensitivity must check the **specific batch allergen label**, not rely on seller assurances.

## 10. Kimmy — conversation policies and live-selling response patterns
